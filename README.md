FAN FORM NOTE
The form validates name, email and favourite format. Successful registrations are saved in localStorage in the same browser/device only. They are NOT uploaded to a shared online database and are not sent to the project team. To collect entries from visitors on different devices, a hosted backend/database is required.

IMAGE NOTE
The two cricket gallery images are AI-generated decorative artwork. The assignment asks for original photos captured by your group; add your own camera photos of a cricket ground, bat/ball or activity (with permission) before final submission. Do not present generated artwork as personally captured photographs.

MATCH DATA NOTE
Fixtures are sample/demo entries, not live scores or confirmed upcoming matches.
